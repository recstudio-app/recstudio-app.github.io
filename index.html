Recstudio

Recstudio is an Android app for watching and managing live TV and video streams. Users can browse channel playlists, search by country and category, and play streams in a built-in player.

The app uses the TMDB API to show movie and TV metadata such as posters, titles, overviews and ratings. TMDB data is only displayed in the app and is not stored or redistributed.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
